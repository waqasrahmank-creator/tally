Tally

Scan a receipt

Take a photo or pick one from your gallery. Tally reads it on your phone and sorts each item into a category. Check the result before saving; your corrections teach it for next time.

Photos never leave your phone. The first scan downloads the text reader (a few MB); after that it’s quicker.
